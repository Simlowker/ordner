**Reuse (to decide before publishing the repo).** All sources are public, freely downloadable official publications (federal, cantonal) or an eCH standard. Only eCH grants an explicit redistribution licence. The admin.ch and gr.ch legal notices require prior written consent for any reproduction; art. 5 para. 1 let. c URG/LDA may exempt the federal circular (report of a public administration) but probably not the cantonal guides. `passages.jsonl` holds the full text of the selected pages, kept so that every quote shown to the user can be checked word for word against its source. Safe option for a public repository: keep only the eCH passages (or none) in git and rebuild the rest locally with the command above (URLs and page lists are in `src/ordner/explain/corpus.py`).

## What is versioned

Only `passages_public.jsonl` (eCH-0196 — redistribution allowed with attribution — and the ESTV/AFC circular 2-215,
an official federal publication, art. 5 al. 1 LDA/URG) is in the repository. Cantonal guide passages (Bern, Ticino,
Graubünden) are not redistributed: `python -m ordner.explain.corpus --download` rebuilds the full `passages.jsonl`
locally from the official URLs above. Precomputed explanations in `data/demo/` contain only short attributed quotations.
